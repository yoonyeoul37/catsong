Privacy Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 28, 2026

KNEXM Co., Ltd. (hereinafter "Company") values the privacy of Paransori app (hereinafter "App") users and complies with applicable privacy laws and regulations.

1. Information We Collect

The App uses only the minimum necessary information as follows:

  • Device storage access permission (to read music files)
  • Media file access permission (album art, video files)
  • In-app settings (theme color, text size, favorites, sound mix settings, etc. – stored on device only)
  • Internet access permission (for streaming playback of radio, nature sounds, and sleep/focus content)
  • Camera permission (used only to control the flashlight; the App does not take or store photos or videos)
  • System settings permission (ringtone setting feature)
  • Bluetooth connection permission (connecting Bluetooth headphones, speakers, and other audio devices)
  • Background playback permissions (foreground service, keeping the screen awake, battery optimization exemption)

The Company does not collect personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.).

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

  • Storage permission is used to play music/video files stored on your device within the App.
  • Internet access is used to stream radio, nature sounds, and sleep/focus content, and to display broadcast schedule information.
  • Camera permission is used only to turn the flashlight on and off.
  • Collected information is not used for any purpose other than providing app functionality.

3. Retention Period

App settings (favorites, playlists, etc.) are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to the Company's servers. All data will be deleted from your device when you uninstall the App.

Sound files you have played once (such as nature sounds) may be temporarily stored (cached) on your device for later playback. They are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear the App's cache.

4. Third-Party Sharing

The Company does not share your personal information with third parties. Exceptions may occur only when required by law.

5. Internet Access and Lyrics Feature

The App may send song titles and artist names to an external API (lrclib.net) for the lyrics search feature. This information is used solely for lyrics retrieval and is not stored.

6. Radio Streaming Feature

The App provides live streaming of domestic and international radio stations.

  • An internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data) is required for radio playback, and data charges may apply.
  • Radio stream addresses and broadcast information are provided by individual broadcasters or external services such as Radio Browser (radio-browser.info).
  • Broadcast schedule information (program names and times) is provided by each broadcaster's public data and by schedule files published on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com).
  • Favorited radio stations and recently played history are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to the Company's servers.
  • The Company is not responsible for the content of radio broadcasts themselves.

7. Nature Sounds, Sound Mix, and Sleep/Focus Content

The App provides audio content such as nature sounds, sound mixes, and sleep/focus music.

  • This content is streamed over the internet from a cloud service used by the Company (Supabase; server location: Tokyo, Japan). An internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data) is required, and data charges may apply.
  • When content is requested, access records (such as IP address and request time) may be generated automatically in the course of operating the service. The Company does not use this information to identify users or for any other purpose.
  • Sounds you have played once may be temporarily stored on your device for later playback, and are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear the App's cache.
  • Sound mixes you create (selected music, nature sound combinations, volume settings, etc.) are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to the Company's servers.

8. Your Rights

  • You can delete stored data (favorites, playlists, etc.) at any time through the App's settings.
  • Uninstalling the App will delete all app data stored on your device.
  • You can grant or revoke the App's permissions (camera, storage, etc.) at any time in your device settings, and you can delete temporarily stored sound files by clearing the App's cache.
